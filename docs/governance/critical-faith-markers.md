## Definitions of the Supreme Deity: Yahweh

Language people may use while describing Yahweh:
1. Higher power
2. Cosmic force
3. Creator
4. Deity

## Draft Markers (Input Area)

1. Clear awareness of a supreme Deity in and around life.
2. Ability to articulate how the Deity is engaging them personally.
3. Ability to identify moments of surrendering personal will to the Deity.
4. Observable evidence of putting others before self.
5. Ability to identify common ground between lived experience and Christ's teaching on the Kingdom (without centering differences).
6. Ability to recognize and categorize Kingdom activities in daily life.
7. Willingness to receive and honor the testimony of others' authentic faith.
8. Ability to recognize depth of faith in self and others.
    - Working definition: depth of faith = authentic conviction expressed in trust, obedience, and relational movement toward God.
9. Shift from conceptual faith language to personal faith language.
10. Faith becomes a normative part of daily life, not an occasional category.
11. Desire for a deeper personal relationship with the Deity.

## Journey Shift Markers

When a person reaches a meaningful shift point, look for:
- More meaningful structure in their faith journey.
- Increasing influence of trusted people.
- Increasing influence of a trusted community.
- Recognition that connection to Jesus changes understanding and direction.
- Beginning to frame faith experiences around the teaching of Jesus.
- Clearer understanding of Jesus' connection to the Deity they have been seeking.
