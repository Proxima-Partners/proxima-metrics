# Executive Summary: Qualitative and Quantitative Indices

Purpose: provide a single executive view of all current indices with consistent fields: name, description, how to measure, and weight.

Sources: [2026 annual evaluation](program-annual-evaluation-2026.md) and [qualitative framework v1](qualitative-metrics-framework-v1.md). This summary does not change either governing definition.

## Quantitative Indices (Annual)

Scoring context:
- Quantitative metrics are scored 0-4 and converted to weighted points.
- Weighted points = (score / 4) x weight.
- Total quantitative weight = 100.

| ID | Name | Description | How to Measure | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Authentic Relationship Development | Share of participants showing meaningful relationship-development movement during the year. | Participants with 3+ relationship signals in year / participants with at least 1 relationship signal in year. Signals: conversation, faith interest, exploration, building trust, deepening faith interest. | 10 |
| P2 | Coaching Conversations | Annual volume of distinct coaching conversations. | Total distinct coaching conversations in the calendar year. | 10 |
| P3 | Coaching Level Progression Rate | Share of people who advance to a meaningful next coaching step. | People advancing to next coaching step / people with first coaching contact. | 15 |
| P4 | Active Learning Communities | Number of learning communities that actually ran. | Count of learning communities run in the year. | 10 |
| P5 | Learning Community Participation | Reach across all learning community sessions. | Total participant attendances across all learning community sessions in year. | 10 |
| P6 | Resource Production Velocity | Annual output of practical ministry resources. | Count of micro-resources published in year. | 12 |
| P7 | Resource-to-Engagement Conversion | Share of resource users who take a meaningful next step. | Resource consumers taking a next ministry step / total resource consumers. | 13 |
| P8 | City Engagement Touchpoints | Breadth of meaningful city-facing presence. | Count of distinct city engagement events in year. | 12 |
| P9 | Relationship Continuity (30-day) | Follow-through consistency after first contact. | New contacts with follow-up within 30 days / total new contacts. | 8 |

## Qualitative Indices (Q1-Q10)

Scoring context:
- Qualitative metrics are coded 0-3 (0 not observed, 3 strong transferable pattern).
- Qualitative framework v1 is primarily pattern-tracking and interpretation, not a weighted annual index.
- Weight is therefore listed as unweighted in current governance.

| ID | Name | Description | How to Measure | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Listening-First Posture | Evidence that listening and questions come before advice or proclamation. | Code monthly evidence rows (notes, transcripts, stories) using rubric 0-3 based on observed listening-first behavior. | Unweighted (v1) |
| Q2 | Discernment Responsiveness | Evidence of Spirit-led adaptation rather than rigid scripting. | Code for discernment pauses, prayer references, and adaptive next steps using rubric 0-3. | Unweighted (v1) |
| Q3 | Grace-Centered Gospel Framing | Evidence that witness language is grace-centered and non-coercive. | Code source excerpts for grace/service/Christ-centered framing using rubric 0-3. | Unweighted (v1) |
| Q4 | Relational Continuity Depth | Evidence of repeated, deepening relational presence over time. | Code repeated touchpoints with specific people/contexts using rubric 0-3. | Unweighted (v1) |
| Q5 | Kingdom-Sign Recognition | Evidence of naming visible good and linking it to Christ's reign. | Code explicit kingdom-sign interpretation language in evidence sources using rubric 0-3. | Unweighted (v1) |
| Q6 | Service-Shaped Leadership | Evidence of humble, service-first leadership posture. | Code for service/humility actions and language over control dynamics using rubric 0-3. | Unweighted (v1) |
| Q7 | Community Belonging Formation | Evidence that trusted community is shaping next faithful steps. | Code for belonging/relational influence signals in stories and notes using rubric 0-3. | Unweighted (v1) |
| Q8 | Faith-Language Personalization | Evidence of shift from abstract spirituality to personal faith language. | Code language movement toward personal God/Jesus engagement using rubric 0-3. | Unweighted (v1) |
| Q9 | Resilient Dependence Under Pressure | Evidence of faithful mission presence during pressure or scarcity. | Code sources for faithfulness under constraints and uncertainty using rubric 0-3. | Unweighted (v1) |
| Q10 | Contextual Witness Fit | Evidence of contextual adaptation without theological drift. | Code for context-aware engagement and language adaptation with conviction integrity using rubric 0-3 plus contradiction checks. | Unweighted (v1) |

## Notes for Executive Use

- Quantitative indices provide weighted annual performance scoring.
- Qualitative indices provide pattern depth, signal quality, and narrative interpretation.
- Read together, they show both measurable outcomes and formation-level quality.
