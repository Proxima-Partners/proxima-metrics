# Biblical Theological Convictions (From Journal Entries)

This list is derived from the two journal files only, with biblical references included wherever the entries explicitly supplied them.

## Governance Instructions

These convictions are the authoritative theological baseline for this project.

- No future precept, framework, metric, interpretation, or recommendation may contradict these convictions.
- All future development work must cross-reference this list before being accepted as project guidance.
- If a proposed statement appears to conflict with this baseline, the proposal must be revised or rejected.
- Any future expansion of theological precepts must explicitly align with this baseline and include supporting references.
- This file functions as the project's theological guardrail for all subsequent documentation and decision-making.

1. **There is one living, supreme God, and worship of substitutes is futile/idolatrous.**
   - References: Acts 14:15-17 (context in entries), Acts 17:24-25, Acts 17:28-29, Exodus 20:3

2. **Jesus is mediator, and the gospel cannot be blended into plural belief systems.**
   - References: Mark 1:15, John 3:3-5

3. **Salvation is through the grace of the Lord Jesus, not ethnic boundary markers or law-keeping.**
   - References: Acts 15:5, Acts 15:10-11

4. **Entry into the Kingdom is by new birth of the Spirit, not human control.**
   - References: John 3:3-5

5. **The Kingdom is already present in Christ and not yet complete.**
   - References: Mark 1:15, Matthew 6:10, Romans 8:18-25

6. **Future hope is resurrection, renewal of creation, and full participation in God’s reign.**
   - References: 1 Corinthians 15:42-57, Revelation 21:1-5, Ephesians 1:13-14

7. **Believers are empowered now to live Kingdom-shaped lives (mercy, truth, righteousness, peace, love).**
   - References: John 17:4, Romans 14:17, Matthew 5:14-16, Philippians 3:20

8. **God’s common kindness means real goodness can appear in a fallen world, but Kingdom fullness is tied to repentance and faith.**
   - References: Genesis 1:26-27, Matthew 5:45, Acts 14:16-17, Romans 2:14-15, John 3:3-5, Mark 1:15

9. **Faithful witness rejects manipulation and leaves outcomes to God.**
   - References: 2 Corinthians 4:2, 1 Thessalonians 2:3-5, 1 Corinthians 3:6-7

10. **The Christian task is to reconnect visible good to its true source and fulfillment in Christ.**
   - References: Matthew 5:16, Matthew 6:10, 1 Corinthians 15:24-26

11. **Disappointment and lament can be voiced directly to God without abandoning faith.**
   - References: Habakkuk 1:2

12. **God’s holiness exposes unrighteousness and calls for repentance and transformed response.**
   - References: Habakkuk 1 (context in entry)

13. **Guidance is relational and often given as needed, by the Holy Spirit, not as total pre-mapped control.**
   - References: Acts 16:6-8, John 14:26, John 16:13, Galatians 5:25, Matthew 6:34

14. **Waiting on the Lord is active trust, not passive inactivity.**
   - References: Psalm 123:2, Psalm 37:7

15. **God appoints times and places so people may seek Him; seeking God is universal to human existence.**
   - References: Acts 17:26-28, Ecclesiastes 1:14

16. **Human identity is creaturely and dependent: in God we live and move and have our being.**
   - References: Acts 17:28-29

17. **No one can serve both God and money; trust and devotion cannot be divided.**
   - References: Matthew 6:24, Acts 16:19

18. **Suffering and resurrection-pattern (death to life, disaster to reversal) is central to the Messiah and His people.**
   - References: Acts 17:3, John 12:24

19. **God’s mission advances even through conflict, weakness, and human limitation.**
   - References: Acts 15:39-40, Romans 8:28 (quoted in journal reflection as principle)

20. **Church leadership exists for prayerful service, doctrinal care, and unity, not control or status power.**
   - References: Acts 14:23, Acts 15:19-20, Acts 20:28-31

21. **The core struggle is spiritual; deception distorts truth and must be resisted in the Lord’s power.**
   - References: Ephesians 6:10-12, Acts 20:29-31

22. **Kingdom fruit is formed by abiding in Christ over time, not by urgency or productivity pressure.**
   - References: John 15:5, Galatians 5 (fruit of the Spirit theme), Ephesians 5:8-9

23. **Resurrection establishes a new relational reality: through Jesus, disciples are welcomed into filial intimacy with the Father.**
   - References: John 20:17, John 15:15, John 20:26

24. **Mission is sustained by God’s presence and promise, not by visible certainty of outcomes.**
   - References: Acts 18:9-10, Matthew 28:20

25. **Neighbor-love in ordinary life is integral to gospel witness.**
   - References: Matthew 22:37-39 (quoted in entry language), Matthew 5:14-16
