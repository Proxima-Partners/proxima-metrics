


VALUE ORIENTATIONS
These are overarching value orientations that inform our culture and guide our decisions, no matter where we work or what we do.
A KINGDOM ORIENTATION                                                                                                        We seek to advance the Kingdom of God above our own brand of Christianity, personal preferences, or organizational success. Our focus is on enriching the broader Christian ecosystem through our unique contributions.
A LOCAL CHURCH ORIENTATION Our role in God’s Kingdom is to extend and strengthen the reach of the local church in post-Christian contexts by utilizing our spiritual gifts, expertise, and experience. While our work may not always directly result in church growth or multiplication, we always strive to enrich and enhance the impact of local congregations.
A RELATIONAL ORIENTATION Loving God and others is central to our mission. We prioritize relationships–both with those we serve and those we serve alongside–-ensuring that love and genuine concern for others remain at the heart of our work, decisions and structures (Philippians 2).
A LEARNING AND GROWTH ORIENTATION In the evolving, post-Christian world, continual learning is vital. We strive to understand the cultural forces shaping people’s lives, staying adaptable and responsive as we integrate what we know. We never stop growing in knowledge, skill, and heart.
A RISK ORIENTATION Guided by biblical wisdom and the Holy Spirit, we serve in faith, trusting God to provide as we take intentional, calculated risks for His glory. We reject the notion that safety, the status quo and resource security are our highest priorities. We seek to be good stewards of the opportunities and resources God gives us.
A CONTEXTUAL ORIENTATION We approach every endeavor with deep respect for local contexts. Rather than imposing external strategies, we listen, observe, and collaborate to honor the unique ways God is already at work in each place. 
A PARTNERSHIP ORIENTATION We seek mutuality in partnerships, establishing rhythms and best practices that reflect our organizational values. We take a redemptive (not transactional), approach to any endeavor. Whenever possible, we work with and through others, avoiding redundancy and ensuring sustainability.

SAN FRANCISCO: DRIVING VALUES
   These are values specific to the work in San Francisco.

	•	LOVE  We care deeply about those far from Christ because God does. We love because He is Love. There is no greater strategy or more authentic approach than following God’s command to love Him and love our neighbors. We relish the opportunities to bring unity around Christian mission and a shared heart for “the one.” (Luke 15)
	•	ABUNDANCE  We rely on God’s provision through faith and operate with a generosity of spirit. We fight isolation, scarcity, competition, cowardice and spiritual battles through community and interdependence.
	•	PROCESS  God is already showing His love to people, and we have the privilege of joining His redemptive work in their lives. We come alongside people who are in process by following the Spirit’s leading. We also invite God to work in our own lives as we learn from others and point them to the grace of Jesus.
	•	UNIQUENESS  We affirm the giftedness of every believer, embracing the freedom to reflect Christ in our own distinct ways. The body of Christ is best when we empower and unleash believers to live out of their unique calling, strengths, experiences, and personalities.
