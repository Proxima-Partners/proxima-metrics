## About Proxima

San Francisco is a place of innovation, diversity, spiritual curiosity, and deep complexity. Proxima Partners generates fresh insights from lived experience in the city, helping Christians navigate cultural tensions, engage people across differences, and reflect Christ in ways that enrich the broader community.

Our aim is to contribute to a more relationally connected, spiritually attentive, and compassionate San Francisco—one where love is visible, faith is authentic, and the common good is pursued so all can flourish.

Proxima Partners helps San Francisco flourish spiritually by equipping Christians to engage the city with the love of Jesus. Through personalized coaching, learning communities, practical resources, and city engagement, we support individuals and leaders in a post-Christian context so they contribute to the well-being of their neighborhoods, workplaces, and relationships with wisdom, humility, and authenticity.

At the heart of our work is helping individuals recognize where God is already present and active around them. Rather than relying on formulas or prescriptive approaches, we cultivate outreach practices that rely on active listening, thoughtful reflection, and relational presence.

We believe lasting impact grows when Christians learn to join what God is already doing in the lives of people, in the life of the city—and respond intuitively with His love.

## Driving Values

### Love
We care deeply about those far from Christ because God does. We move beyond fear toward a more authentic approach than simply following God’s command to love Him and love our neighbors. We allow opportunities to bring unity around Christian mission and shared heart for “the one.”

### Abundance
We rely on God’s provision through faith and operate with a generous spirit. We fight isolation, scarcity, competition, and cowardice—as well as spiritual battles—through community and interdependence.

### Process
God is already showing His love to people, and we have the privilege of joining His redemptive work in their lives. We come alongside people who are in process by following the Spirit’s leading. We also invite God to work in our own lives as we learn from others and point them to the grace of Jesus.

### Uniqueness
We affirm the giftedness of every believer and embrace the freedom to reflect Christ in our distinct ways. The body of Christ is best when we empower and unleash believers to live out their unique callings, strengths, experiences, and personalities.

## Message from Program Director (Mike Edwards)

Your support, belief, and faithful commitment to the birth of Proxima is invaluable. Thank you.

Looking back to the beginning of the year, there is a sense that everything changed and nothing changed. Our ministry remains focused on San Francisco, and our relationships remain similar—but the structure around us has shifted significantly.

The path forward has not been direct. It has required navigating uncertainty—not in quantity of time or length of insight, but through depth of relationship with Jesus and clarity about how to serve others.

## Lessons Learned

### 1. Credibility Hinges on Lived Experience
San Francisco does not trust Christian influence built on words. Credibility is built through actions. When our confidence is rooted in Christ rather than self-serving outcomes, it becomes a lived expression of trust.

### 2. The Context Demands Gospel Clarity
In a city shaped by innovation, spirituality, and skepticism, the Gospel must remain clear and centered on Jesus’ authority and grace.

### 3. Long-Term Relationships Matter
In a transient, achievement-driven environment, influence grows through patient presence. Faithfulness over time speaks louder than visible momentum.

### 4. Lived Experience Engages More Than Debate
Authenticity and humility foster deeper conversations than rehearsed arguments.

### 5. Contentment Counters Achievement-Driven Identity
San Francisco’s culture of performance shapes identity. Contentment in Christ provides an alternative rooted in confidence without effort and abundance without limit.

### 6. Spiritual Diversity Requires Strong Theology
San Francisco’s spiritual plurality requires unwavering trust in Jesus as Lord while engaging respectfully with diverse beliefs.
