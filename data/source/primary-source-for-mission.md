# Primary Source for Mission — Strategy — Plans

**Tag Line:** Extending God's Love through Coaching

---

## Mission Statement

Proxima Partners helps people with spiritual interest navigate the diversity of faith in San Francisco to discover the love of Jesus in the context of responsive coaching, learning communities, and narrative testimonies.

---

## What We Do

Proxima offers personalized coaching for San Francisco residents navigating a diverse and demanding urban environment. Through one-on-one coaching, we help people understand their unique wiring and how their gifts contribute to the city. We serve people from all backgrounds—Christian, other faith traditions, and those who are unaffiliated. We are passionate about coming alongside people to help them grow, flourish, and live with purpose in the city they call home. Reach out and let's see what we discover together!
