# Proxima Zoom Update --- Cleaned Transcript

## Opening (Katie)

Thank you all for joining us. We're excited to share what we're learning
as we come alongside people in San Francisco to reflect the love of
Christ in everyday life. We'll also share our plans for 2026 and our
current financial needs.

San Francisco is often misunderstood. Many Christians have written it
off as too difficult or too different. But what we're seeing is that
**God is still at work here**. The story of this city is not over.

Sometimes we hear dramatic testimony stories that can actually feel
discouraging if we don't see that same kind of transformation in our own
lives. What we're learning is that God often works in quieter, subtler
ways.

In Berlin, we used to say: **"Celebrate the millimeters."** Growth is
not always linear. The key is learning to notice where God is already at
work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Story: Mickey (Katie)

A friend of mine---I'll call him Mickey---had an argument in a group
setting and stormed off. It was disruptive and embarrassing.

A few weeks later, he came back.

Not only that---he apologized to everyone involved. It was heartfelt,
intentional, and required real courage.

He could have avoided us. He could have ignored it. But instead, he
chose to repair what was broken.

I told him how much that meant and how much I respected him for it. His
eyes welled up.

That moment wasn't a conversion story. But it was clear:

> **God was at work.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Story: Unhoused Friend (Mike)

One of my closest friends in the city is unhoused. When I first met him,
he said he didn't believe in anything---not even atheism.

A year later, he texted me saying he was praying for me.

At first, he said he only prayed because he saw Christians do it. But
recently, after someone gave him money when he needed it, he told me:

> "God was providing for me."

If I push too hard, he pulls back. But he's moving.

That's the point.

> **Look for where God is working---not for immediate results.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Relational Lens (Katie)

A common question is: *How long do you stay in relationship with someone
who isn't interested in Christianity?*

Our response:

-   God is patient with us\
-   People are in process\
-   Individuals are worth loving

Sometimes discouragement says more about us than about them.

We don't always know our role in someone's story. Most of us enter in
the middle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## God's Story (Mike)

We need a reset:

> **This is not our story. It's God's story.**

We are participants, not authors.

If we think it depends on us, we'll get discouraged. But if we trust
that God is working, we can simply join Him.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Practical Posture

Katie: "Show up and let God do the rest."

Mike: It's not passive.

It requires: - Listening deeply\
- Putting others first\
- Asking the Holy Spirit what to do in the moment

> **You engage God for the sake of the other person.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Being "For" People (Katie)

The question becomes:

> **How can I be for this person?**

Not before or after---but in the moment.

This requires: - Sensitivity\
- Patience\
- Vulnerability

Example: Walking with a friend struggling with addiction by simply being
present and sharing honestly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Cultural Shift (Mike)

We are living in a moment where:

-   Information is everywhere\
-   Truth is contested\
-   Words carry less meaning

Because of this:

> **People don't trust words---they trust lived experience.**

The "evidence" today is not arguments---it's **our lives**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Proxima Focus

We exist to help Christians: - Learn God's love\
- Live in God's love\
- Be God's love

Our approach is: - Relational (not curriculum-driven)\
- Contextual (not one-size-fits-all)\
- Personalized (based on each person's wiring)
