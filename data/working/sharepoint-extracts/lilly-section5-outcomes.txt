# Proposal Narrative — Section 5: Outcomes, Performance Indicators, and Evaluation
## Proxima Partners | Lilly Endowment Exploring Christian Practices Initiative

---

We want to be honest about something before naming our outcomes. The practices at the center of this program — discernment, faith-sharing, testimony — produce the kind of growth that is real but rarely linear. A person sitting with a question they have never had language for before is experiencing something significant. A learning community where someone says out loud, for the first time, what they actually believe — that is a moment with weight. These are not events that show up cleanly in a spreadsheet. They are the millimeters of spiritual formation that accumulate, over time, into genuine change.

We hold that reality alongside our conviction that good stewardship requires accountability. We are committed to tracking our progress rigorously — quantitatively where the data is meaningful, qualitatively where numbers alone would flatten what is actually happening. Both kinds of evidence matter, and our evaluation plan is designed to gather both.

---

### Outcome 1: Increased Access to Discernment Coaching

**Statement:** Individuals from all three target populations — unaffiliated, nominally connected, and committed Christians — participate in structured discernment coaching and report growth in spiritual clarity, sense of purpose, and capacity to engage their faith in daily life.

**Performance Indicators:**
- Number of individuals served through individual coaching in each program year
- Number of coaching sessions completed per participant
- Percentage of participants completing a full coaching engagement (as defined by the program's introductory and unscripted tracks)
- Percentage of participants who, in a post-engagement survey, report increased spiritual clarity, clearer sense of calling or purpose, or greater confidence engaging spiritual questions in their relationships

### Outcome 2: Deepened Faith and Community Through Learning Communities

**Statement:** Participants in cohort-based learning communities report deepened faith, stronger sense of Christian community, and growth in the specific practices — discernment, faith-sharing, testimony — introduced in the group setting.

### Outcome 3: Practices Extended Into Participants' Relational Networks

**Statement:** Participants who have engaged Proxima's coaching and learning community work naturally carry these practices — discernment conversations, faith-sharing, testimony — into their own relational networks, extending access to Christian practices beyond what Proxima can deliver directly.

**Performance Indicators:**
- Number of coaching participants and learning community graduates who report initiating spiritual conversations or discernment-style conversations with others in their networks
- Number of individuals trained as learning community facilitators
- Number of new relationships or settings where Christian practices are being extended by Proxima participants, as documented through annual follow-up
- Qualitative accounts from participants describing specific moments where they applied a practice learned through Proxima in a relationship outside the program

### Outcome 4: Insights That Strengthen the Broader Urban Ministry Field

**Statement:** Proxima documents, refines, and shares what it is learning about which Christian practices work in post-Christian urban contexts and why — contributing actionable knowledge to urban ministries nationally and equipping them to adapt these practices in their own cities.

**Performance Indicators:**
- Number of micro-resources produced and distributed annually
- Number of unique users engaging Proxima's insights platform
- Number of formal or informal knowledge-sharing engagements with other urban ministries (conversations, presentations, collaborations)
- Evidence that peer organizations have adopted or adapted Proxima's documented practices or methodologies, as gathered through qualitative feedback and direct engagement

### Evaluation Plan

**Quantitative Tracking**
Katie and Mike will maintain a simple, consistent tracking system across all program activity — coaching sessions, learning community meetings, testimony gatherings — recording participant counts, engagement levels, and completion rates. This data will be reviewed quarterly and reported to the board and to Lilly annually.

**Pre- and Post-Surveys**
Each coaching engagement and learning community cohort will include a brief intake survey and a closing survey. Surveys will ask participants to reflect on their spiritual clarity, sense of community, comfort with specific practices, and openness to continued engagement. We will consult with evaluation professionals to identify or adapt validated instruments appropriate to our population and context, drawing from the field of spiritual formation research.

**Qualitative Documentation**
Katie and Mike will maintain ongoing narrative documentation of what they are observing in coaching and community work — not as a formal research project, but as the same reflective practice that produced the lessons learned already evident in the 2025 Annual Report. These notes will feed into annual program assessments and provide the thick description that quantitative data alone cannot supply.

**Follow-Up Check-Ins**
Three to six months after completing a coaching engagement or learning community cohort, participants will be contacted for a brief follow-up conversation or survey. This follow-up is specifically designed to track whether practices introduced through Proxima have continued and spread — addressing Outcome 3 directly.

**Annual Program Review**
Each year, Katie and Mike will conduct a formal program review with the advisory board, presenting quantitative data, qualitative findings, and recommended adjustments. This review will ensure that the program is learning from itself and adapting in real time to what the city and its people are actually showing us.
