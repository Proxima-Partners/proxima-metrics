## 2025-09-24 — What Brought Me to Tears

**From:** Mike Edwards (mike.edwards@liveproxima.org)
**To:** Proxima Partners mailing list

Mike's September 2025 newsletter. Theme: "From Third Person to First Name."

Mike reflects on the tendency to speak about people in abstract categories (the homeless, the LGBTQ+ community, liberals) rather than as individuals. In prayer one morning, the Holy Spirit replaced those categories with actual faces and names:

- Ben, Mud, Dino, Hank, Patti — people experiencing homelessness
- Tim, Sandra, Edward — LGBTQ+ neighbors
- Frank, Teressa, Naomi — neighbors with progressive political views

Mike wept and repented — not for caring about these communities in the abstract, but for holding them at arm's length. The moment marked a shift in how he approaches his presence and prayer in San Francisco.

Financial update: Over 70% of former EFCA giving partners have transferred their support to Proxima. EFCA ReachGlobal accounts now closed.

Upcoming: September 30 Zoom update.
