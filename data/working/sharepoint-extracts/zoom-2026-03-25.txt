# Proxima Zoom Update --- Cleaned Transcript

*(Theme: Every Moment Matters)*

## Opening & Context (Katie)

Thank you so much for joining us for our first Zoom update of the year.
We're excited to share what we're seeing God doing in San Francisco as
we interact with people in the city. Our theme today is **"Every Moment
Matters."**

We'll share a few stories and also respond to questions many of you
submitted. Mike and I are both in a very active learning phase---we're
experimenting, trying to understand the city, people's beliefs, and
where Christians get stuck in reflecting God's love.

Proxima was started in 2025, so this is our first full year. We're very
much in a startup phase, building foundations. That's why learning is so
important---we want to create programming that actually meets people
where they are.

San Francisco is unique. While some see it as an outlier, historically
it's more of a bellwether---it often shapes what others will believe
later. So we're focused on helping people **navigate the spirituality of
this city**.

What we've discovered is that *everyone*---regardless of belief---needs
help navigating this.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Theological Shift (Mike)

Jesus is Lord over all---fully, completely.

If that's true, then when people pursue spirituality, ultimately those
paths lead toward God. So instead of negating what people are doing, we
can actually **encourage their spiritual pursuit**, trusting that God is
the true endpoint.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Relational Approach (Katie)

We don't divide people into categories. We're here to help *everyone*
navigate spirituality.

Our work is primarily relational: - Friendship\
- Listening\
- Asking good questions\
- Creating space

Workshops help, but what people need most is **personal engagement**.
