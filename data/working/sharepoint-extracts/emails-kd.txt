## 2025-11-25 — What We Often Overlook

**From:** Constant Contact campaign on behalf of Proxima Partners
**To:** Proxima Partners mailing list

Narrative newsletter written by Katie.

Katie describes walking to a pancake breakfast at Golden Gate Park — a moment of personal spiritual examination. She questions her own motives: Is she doing this out of genuine love, or is she performing? Along the way, she witnesses a tense conflict between two people that de-escalates. She realizes her role is not to engineer outcomes but to align herself with God's presence, which is already active in the city.

The "what we often overlook" theme: God is already present and working; our job is to notice and join Him.

Closes with a year-end giving appeal.
